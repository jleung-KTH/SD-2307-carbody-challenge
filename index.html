    Design review

    Mr. ScharfenbergChief carbody reviewer. Couples automatically, never loosely.

    Ask for a review when you have a first version. Mr. Scharfenberg reads the brief, your decisions and the estimates, tells you what holds, what is loose, and the one change he would make next.

    Estimates come from a simplified teaching model built on the lecture's rules of thumb (passenger flow per lane, floor-height build-up, EN 12663 payload, curve throw, crash energy). They are for comparing concepts, not for sizing a real car. Brief values are exercise assumptions.
